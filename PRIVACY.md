# Privacy Policy — Spot2About

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Spot2About is a free browser extension that automatically displays your currently playing Spotify song in your WhatsApp About.

This policy explains how Spot2About handles information and what permissions it requires.

## 2. Information We Access

Spot2About accesses:

- **Spotify Web Player:** Song titles, artist names, and playback status.
- **WhatsApp Web:** The interface required to update your WhatsApp About.
- **Browser tabs:** Information needed to detect whether Spotify Web Player and WhatsApp Web are open.

These permissions are used to provide the extension's core functionality.

## 3. Information Stored

Spot2About uses your browser's local extension storage to save:

- Whether automatic syncing is enabled.
- Your custom no-music message.
- The last song or status sent.
- The status that should appear in your WhatsApp About.

This information is stored locally in your browser.

## 4. Data Collection and Sharing

Spot2About does not operate its own analytics or tracking service.

The extension does not intentionally collect or send personal data to a developer-operated server.

Song and artist information is used to update your WhatsApp About through WhatsApp Web.

Spotify and WhatsApp may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. WhatsApp and Spotify

Spot2About works with Spotify Web Player and WhatsApp Web.

It is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially supported by Spotify or WhatsApp.

The extension does not require you to provide your Spotify or WhatsApp passwords directly to Spot2About.

## 6. Donations

Spot2About provides an optional link to Ko-fi for users who wish to support the project.

Donations are voluntary and are not required to use the extension.

If you visit Ko-fi, its own privacy policy applies.

## 7. Data Retention and Removal

Stored settings and status information can be cleared through your browser's extension storage controls.

Uninstalling Spot2About generally removes its locally stored extension data.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Spot2About gains new features or changes how it handles information.

Updates will be published in this GitHub repository.
